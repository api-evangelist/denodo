Composable Systems is a Denodo implementation partner specializing in data virtualization for banking and public sector clients in Saudi Arabia. The partner deploys logical data platforms that unify siloed data while meeting local regulatory standards including SAMA, PDPL, and NDMO frameworks, enabling unified, governed, real-time data access without disrupting existing infrastructure.
