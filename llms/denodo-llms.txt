# Denodo

> Denodo is the AI data layer company. The award-winning Denodo Platform powers trustworthy agents and applications, transforming enterprise data into reliable insights for analytics and self-service. Organizations worldwide deploy Denodo alongside their data lakehouses to deliver AI-ready, business-ready data in a fraction of the time.

## Products

- [Denodo Platform](https://www.denodo.com/en/denodo-platform/denodo-platform.md): Denodo's AI Data Layer and logical data management platform for live data access, semantic unification, governance, self-service, analytics, applications, and AI.
- [Denodo Assistant](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-capability/denodo-assistant.md): AI-powered capabilities for conversational data access, recommendations, development assistance, metadata enrichment, and performance optimization.
- [Denodo Data Marketplace](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-capability/data-marketplace.md): Self-service discovery and consumption experience for governed data products, metadata, lineage, business context, access requests, and conversational data exploration.
- [Denodo Scheduler](https://community.denodo.com/docs/html/browse/latest/en/scheduler/administration/introduction/introduction): Schedules and executes time-based data extraction, integration, cache-preloading, and related jobs against Denodo Virtual DataPort.
- [Denodo Design Studio](https://community.denodo.com/docs/html/browse/latest/en/vdp/administration/starting_virtual_dataport/starting_virtual_dataport): The development and modeling environment used to connect data sources, build virtual data models and views, query data, and publish data services.
- [Denodo Solution Manager](https://community.denodo.com/docs/html/browse/latest/en/solution_manager/administration/index): Central administration environment for managing Denodo deployments, environments, clusters, licenses, configuration, authentication, and operational lifecycle tasks.

## Core Topics

- [AI Data Layer](https://www.denodo.com/en/glossary/ai-data-layer-definition-importance-and-key-components.md): A logical data layer that provides AI systems with current, contextualized, semantically consistent, and governed enterprise data across distributed sources.
- [Logical Data Management](https://www.denodo.com/en/data-management/logical-data-management.md): Explains how Denodo unifies and manages distributed enterprise data through a logical layer while preserving live access, business context, security, and governance.
- [Semantic Layer](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-capability/universal-semantic-layer.md): Provides consistent business definitions, relationships, lineage, and semantic context across distributed data for analytics, applications, and AI.
- [Data Products](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-technology/data-products.md): Explains how Denodo helps organizations create, govern, manage, and deliver reusable data products across distributed enterprise data.
- [AI-ready Data](https://www.denodo.com/en/data-management/logical-data-management.md): Shows how logical data management delivers live, authoritative, secure, governed, and business-contextual data for AI applications.
- [Agentic AI](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-technology/agentic-ai.md): Describes how Denodo provides AI agents with live enterprise data, consistent semantics, governance, security, and trusted business context.
- [Data Governance](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-use-case/centralize-data-governance-risk-and-compliance.md): Explains how Denodo centralizes governance and security policies across distributed data while supporting fine-grained, real-time access control.
- [Data Catalog and Marketplace](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-capability/data-marketplace.md): Provides a governed, self-service experience for discovering, understanding, requesting, accessing, and sharing trusted enterprise data products.
- [APIs and Data Services](https://www.denodo.com/en/solutions/by-technology/enterprise-data-services.md): Describes how Denodo exposes governed enterprise data through standard interfaces including SQL, JDBC, ODBC, REST, OData, and GraphQL.
